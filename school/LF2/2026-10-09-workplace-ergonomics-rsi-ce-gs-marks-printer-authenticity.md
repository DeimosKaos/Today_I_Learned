## Key Topics — Hauptthemen

### 1. Workstation Ergonomics & The RSI Syndrome — Ergonomie & RSI-Prävention

**🇬🇧 Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI / "Mausarm"):**
- Micro-trauma caused by continuous, repetitive micro-movements, static wrist flexion, and excessive tension during mouse/keyboard operation.
- **Symptoms:** Numbness, tingling, tendon sheath inflammation (*Sehnenscheidenentzündung*), chronic wrist pain.
- **Preventive Hardware Countermeasures:**
  - **Vertical Ergonomic Mice:** Rotates the forearm into a natural handshake posture, relieving the carpal tunnel.
  - **Split Ergonomic Keyboards:** Prevents ulnar deviation of the wrists.
  - **Workplace Layout:** Monitor top edge aligned with eye height; 90-degree angle for elbows and knees.

### 2. Mandatory Electrical Device Labeling — Gesetzliche Kennzeichnungspflichten

| Certification Mark / Symbol | Full Meaning / Name | Legal Significance / Rechtliche Bedeutung |
|---|---|---|
| **CE Mark** | *Conformité Européenne* | **Mandatory self-declaration by manufacturer:** Confirms product meets all applicable European health, safety, and environmental directives. Without CE, products cannot be legally sold in the EU. |
| **GS Mark** | *Geprüfte Sicherheit* (Tested Safety) | **Voluntary safety mark verified by an accredited independent test lab** (e.g. TÜV, VDE): Certifies full compliance with the German Product Safety Act (*Produktsicherheitsgesetz / ProdSG*). |
| **WEEE (Crossed Wheelie Bin)** | *Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment* | Prohibits disposing of electronics in municipal household trash; mandates manufacturer take-back and specialized recycling according to the German ElektroG. |

### 3. Printing Technologies & Document Authenticity — Druckerarten & DIN 12757-2

| Printer Technology | Printing Mechanism / Funktionsweise | Primary Enterprise Use Case |
|---|---|---|
| **Inkjet (Tintenstrahl)** | Liquid ink droplets fired via piezo or thermal bubble-jet nozzles. | Low-volume office color printing, photo printing. Risk of dried nozzles if idle. |
| **Laser Printer** | Laser beam discharges electrostatic drum; attracts dry toner powder; fused by heated roller (*Fuser*). | High-volume corporate document printing, fast text output, smudge-proof. |
| **LED Printer** | Employs a solid-state LED bar instead of rotating polygon laser mirrors. | More compact than laser, fewer moving optical components, high mechanical reliability. |
| **Thermal Printer (Direct/Transfer)** | Heat-sensitive paper turns black under heated printhead (Direct) or melts wax/resin ribbon (Transfer). | POS receipts, barcode labels, logistics parcel tracking labels. |
| **Dot-Matrix / Needle (Nadeldrucker)** | Mechanical pins strike an inked ribbon against paper. | **Carbon copies (Durchschläge)** in logistics, shipping delivery manifests, continuous feed forms. |

**Document Authenticity (Dokumentenechtheit nach DIN 12757-2):**
- **🇬🇧 Requirement:** Official legal documents (notarial deeds, contracts, diplomas, bank guarantees) legally mandate ink/toner certified according to **DIN 12757-2**.
- **Criteria:** Lightfastness (resists fading), water resistance, chemical resistance against erasure/manipulation.
- **Consequence of Non-Compliance:** Legal agreements signed or printed with non-compliant ink can be declared invalid or challenged for falsification in judicial disputes.

## Key Takeaway — Was ich gelernt habe

**🇬🇧**
- **CE is a manufacturer promise, GS is an independent proof:** The CE mark is self-declared; the GS mark provides proof of testing by an accredited authority like TÜV.
- **Document authenticity is a strict legal barrier:** In business process administration, official records and invoices must be processed using DIN 12757-2 certified toner or ink to guarantee tamper-proof audit trails.

**🇩🇪**
- **CE ist eine Eigenerklärung, GS ein unabhängiges Prüfsiegel:** Das CE-Zeichen deklariert der Hersteller selbst; das GS-Zeichen belegt die reale Prüfung durch anerkannte Prüfstellen (z. B. TÜV).
- **Dokumentenechtheit schützt vor Rechtsunwirksamkeit:** Verträge und behördliche Schriftstücke erfordern zwingend nach DIN 12757-2 zertifizierte Toner oder Tinten, um Revisionssicherheit vor Gericht zu gewährleisten.
