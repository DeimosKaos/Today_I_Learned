## Key Topics — Hauptthemen

### 1. What is Computer Science? — Was ist Informatik?

**🇬🇧** Computer Science (*Informatik*) is the systematic science of automated information processing, representation, storage, transmission, and computation using algorithmic systems.
**🇩🇪** Die Informatik ist die Wissenschaft von der systematischen Verarbeitung, Speicherung, Übertragung und Berechnung von Informationen mittels automatisierter, algorithmischer Systeme.

---

### 2. Historical Milestones of Computing — Meilensteine der Computergeschichte

| Era / Epoche | Pioneer / Technology | Key Milestone / Bedeutung |
|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical Era (17th–19th C.)** | Wilhelm Schickard, Blaise Pascal, Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, Charles Babbage | Mechanical clockwork gear calculators and the concept of the *Analytical Engine* (programmable computing). |
| **Relay & Electro-Mechanical (1930s–40s)** | Konrad Zuse (Z3, 1941) | World's first functional programmable, fully automatic digital computer based on binary floating-point. |
| **Vacuum Tubes (1st Gen, 1940s–50s)** | ENIAC, John von Neumann | Electronic computing; formulation of the **Von Neumann Architecture** (CPU, Memory, I/O, Bus). |
| **Transistors & Microchips (2nd–4th Gen)** | Shockley/Bardeen/Brattain, Intel 4004 (1971) | Miniaturization, microprocessors, commercial personal computers, and global networked systems. |

---

### 3. Representation of Information — Darstellung von Informationen

**🇬🇧** Information in digital systems is stripped of physical ambiguity by using discrete mathematical bases:
**🇩🇪** Digitale Systeme stellen Informationen über mathematische Basen diskret dar:

| Numeral System / Zahlensystem | Base / Basis | Digits / Ziffernvorrat | Technical Purpose in Computing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Binary (Dualsystem)** | $2$ | `0`, `1` | Direct mapping to physical transistor states (LOW/HIGH voltage). |
| **Octal (Oktalsystem)** | $8$ | `0`–`7` | Compact grouping of 3 binary bits ($2^3 = 8$), historical file permissions. |
| **Decimal (Dezimalsystem)** | $10$ | `0`–`9` | Human-readable numerical interface. |
| **Hexadecimal (Hexadezimal)** | $16$ | `0`–`9`, `A`–`F` | Compact grouping of 4 binary bits ($1 \text{ Byte} = 2 \text{ Hex digits}$), memory dumps, MAC/IPv6 addresses. |

## Key Takeaway — Was ich gelernt habe

**🇬🇧**
- **Informatik is older than silicon:** The foundational principles of programming and algorithms were established long before electronic microchips were invented.
- **Hexadecimal is human-friendly binary:** Instead of parsing strings of 8 bits like `11011010`, working with `0xDA` drastically reduces human error in low-level engineering.

**🇩🇪**
- **Informatik ist älter als der Silizium-Chip:** Die Grundprinzipien von Algorithmen und Datenverarbeitung entstanden lange vor modernen Mikrochips.
- **Hexadezimal ist lesbares Binär:** Anstelle unübersichtlicher 8-Bit-Ketten wie `11011010` minimiert die Hex-Schreibweise `0xDA` Lesefehler im Systembetrieb.
