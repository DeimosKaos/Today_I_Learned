## Key Topics — Hauptthemen

### 1. Enterprise Resource & Inventory Systems (WWS) — Warenwirtschaftssysteme

**🇬🇧** A **WWS** is an integrated software system that tracks physical goods and financial flows across Purchasing, Warehousing, and Sales:
**🇩🇪** Ein **WWS** steuert und dokumentiert Waren- und Werteflüsse über Einkauf, Lagerhaltung und Verkauf hinweg:

```
[ Einkauf (Purchasing) ] ──► [ Lagerhaltung (Inventory / WWS) ] ──► [ Verkauf (Sales / Billing) ]
```

- **Core Functions:** Automated stock level monitoring, supplier re-ordering triggers, inventory valuation (*Lagerbewertung*), and dispatch management.

---

### 2. Procurement Risks: Maverick Buying — Unkontrollierter Einkauf

**🇬🇧 What is Maverick Buying?**
- When departments or individual employees procure goods, software licenses, or hardware independently without involving the official Purchasing / IT department.
- **Risks for the Enterprise:** Loss of volume discounts (*Mengenrabatte*), duplicate software licenses, shadow IT security risks, incompatible hardware, and untracked expenses.
- **Process Optimization Solution:** Centralized e-procurement approval workflows with role-based access control inside the ERP/WWS.

### 4. Quantitative Market Metrics — Marktpotenzial, Marktvolumen & Marktanteil

**🇬🇧** Quantitative market sizing relies on three nested metrics:
**🇩🇪** Die quantitative Marktanalyse unterscheidet drei ineinandergreifende Größen:

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Marktpotenzial (Market Potential)                        │  ← Theoretical maximum capacity of the market
│   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐   │
│   │  Marktvolumen (Market Volume)                     │   │  ← Actual total real sales of all competitors
│   │   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐   │   │
│   │   │  Marktanteil (Market Share of our firm)   │   │   │  ← Percentage captured by our enterprise
│   │   └───────────────────────────────────────────┘   │   │
│   └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘   │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Formulas & Definitions:**
- **Marktkapazität (Market Capacity):** Theoretical demand assuming goods were free.
- **Marktpotenzial (Market Potential):** Maximum reachable sales volume under current economic conditions and purchasing power.
- **Marktvolumen (Market Volume):** Actual total sales achieved by all market participants combined.
- **Marktanteil (Relative Market Share Formula):**

$$\text{Marktanteil (\%)} = \frac{\text{Umsatz des eigenen Unternehmens}}{\text{Marktvolumen des Gesamtmarktes}} \times 100$$

- **Sättigungsgrad (Market Saturation Degree):**

$$\text{Sättigungsgrad (\%)} = \frac{\text{Marktvolumen}}{\text{Marktpotenzial}} \times 100$$

## Key Takeaway — Was ich gelernt habe

**🇬🇧**
- **WWS integration eliminates shadow IT:** Establishing seamless digital requisition workflows prevents Maverick Buying and protects corporate security policies.
- **Saturation degree drives strategy:** If Market Saturation is $<70\%$, invest in market expansion; if it is near $100\%$ (saturated market), growth is only possible by taking market share from competitors through price or innovation.

**🇩🇪**
- **WWS-Integration verhindert Schatten-IT:** Strukturierte digitale Bestellprozesse unterbinden Maverick Buying und sichern Rabatt- und Compliance-Vorteile.
- **Sättigungsgrad bestimmt die Marktstrategie:** Bei geringem Sättigungsgrad wächst der Markt insgesamt; bei hoher Marktsättigung ($>90\%$) ist Wachstum nur durch Verdrängung von Wettbewerbern möglich.
