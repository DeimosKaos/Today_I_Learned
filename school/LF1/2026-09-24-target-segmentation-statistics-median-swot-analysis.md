## Key Topics — Hauptthemen

### 1. Target Audience Segmentation — Zielgruppenanalyse

| Segmentation Dimension / Kriterium | Specific Attributes / Merkmale | Example Profile for a B2B SaaS Solution |
|---|---|---|
| **Geographic / Geografisch** | Country, region, city size, urban vs. rural / Land, Region, Standort. | DACH region, urban tech hubs (Berlin, Munich, Zurich). |
| **Demographic / Soziodemografisch** | Age, gender, income, education, role / Alter, Beruf, Einkommen. | Decision-makers: IT managers, CIOs, aged 30–55. |
| **Psychographic / Psychografisch** | Values, lifestyle, risk tolerance, tech affinity / Werte, Einstellung. | High affinity for automated workflows, privacy-conscious. |
| **Behavioral / Verhaltensorientiert** | Usage frequency, price sensitivity, brand loyalty / Kaufgewohnheiten. | Daily users, seeking efficiency gains, fast onboarding. |

### 2. Market Research Methods — Primär- vs. Sekundärforschung

| Research Method / Erhebungsart | Data Origin / Herkunft | ✅ Advantages / Vorteile | ❌ Disadvantages / Nachteile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Research (Fieldwork / Primärforschung)** | Newly collected first-hand data for this specific study (surveys, interviews, A/B testing) / Neu erhobene Daten. | Perfectly tailored to the current business question, exclusive insights. | High financial cost, time-intensive data collection. |
| **Secondary Research (Deskwork / Sekundärforschung)** | Analysis of already existing data (Destatis, company reports, academic papers) / Bereits vorhandene Daten. | Fast, inexpensive, broad historical overview. | Not tailored to specific product, potential data age/bias. |

### 3. Descriptive Statistics: Central Tendency Metrics — Statistische Lageparameter

**🇬🇧** When analyzing customer data (e.g., purchase values, user ages), choosing the right central metric prevents distorted conclusions:
**🇩🇪** Bei der Auswertung von Kundendaten ist die Wahl des richtigen Lageparameters entscheidend:

```
Data Sample / Stichprobe: [ 20, 22, 22, 25, 28, 30, 95 ]  (Note the extreme outlier: 95)
```

| Metric / Lageparameter | Formula & Definition | Result on Sample | When to Use / Einsatz |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arithmetischer Mittelwert (Mean)** | $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum x_i}{n} = \frac{242}{7}$ | $\approx \mathbf{34.57}$ | Symmetrical data without extreme outliers. |
| **Median (Zentralwert)** | Middle value of sorted list: position $\frac{n+1}{2}$ | $\mathbf{25.00}$ | **Robust against extreme outliers** (e.g. income/salary data). |
| **Modus / Modalwert (Mode)** | Most frequently occurring value in the dataset | $\mathbf{22.00}$ | Nominal data (e.g. most popular software tier/color). |

### 4. Strategic Planning: SWOT Matrix — SWOT-Analyse

**🇬🇧** SWOT combines internal company capability analysis with external market environmental data (from PESTEL):
**🇩🇪** Die SWOT-Analyse verknüpft interne Stärken/Schwächen mit externen Chancen/Risiken:

| | Helpful to Objective / Positiv | Harmful to Objective / Negativ |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal (Company / Intern)** | **Strengths (Stärken - S):** Proprietary AI algorithms, skilled dev team, high customer trust. | **Weaknesses (Schwächen - W):** Limited marketing budget, legacy technical debt in codebase. |
| **External (Market / Extern)** | **Opportunities (Chancen - O):** Emerging EU AI subsidies, competitors failing to comply with DSGVO. | **Threats (Risiken - T):** Cyber threats, rapid inflation of cloud infrastructure pricing. |

## Key Takeaway — Was ich gelernt habe

**🇬🇧**
- **Median beats Mean when outliers exist:** When reporting user session durations or customer spend, the arithmetic mean is skewed heavily by top spenders — the median provides a realistic picture of typical behavior.
- **SWOT generates actionable strategies:** Combining $S \times O$ creates growth strategies; combining $W \times T$ creates risk defense plans.

**🇩🇪**
- **Der Median ist robuster als der Mittelwert:** Bei verzerrten Verteilungen (z. B. Gehälter oder Sitzungsdauer) verfälschen Ausreißer den Mittelwert — der Median liefert die realistische Mitte.
- **SWOT liefert handlungsleitende Strategien:** Die Kombination aus Stärken und Chancen ($S \times O$) erzeugt Angriffsstrategien; Schwächen und Risiken ($W \times T$) definieren Absicherungsmaßnahmen.
